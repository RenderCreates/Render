A few things
you might be wondering.

Can we start with just one video?+

Yes. We can start with one defined project or build an ongoing partnership around your content and marketing needs.

Can you manage our social media too?+

Yes. We can plan content, create the assets, write captions, schedule posts and review performance. We agree on the channels and responsibilities before starting.

How do you price a project?+

Pricing depends on the scope, production requirements and timeline. Send us a short brief and we’ll discuss the deliverables and prepare a tailored proposal.

What do you need from us to get started?+

Your brand name, a little context about your business, the goal of the project and any timing requirements. If you have brand assets or references, we’d love to see those too.
